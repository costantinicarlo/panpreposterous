# Introduction

Unicode punctuation and symbols should render without manual rewrites:

- Accented Latin letters: é, è, ï, ä, ç, ñ, á, ó, ú, ã, õ, â, ê, ô, ç
- En dash: –
- Em dash: —
- Ellipsis: …
- Minus: −
- Plus/minus: ±
- Multiplication and division: × and ÷
- Comparison operators: ≤, ≥, ≈, ≠
- Arrows: →, ←, ↔, ⇒, ⇐, ⇔
- Micro and degree: 10 µm at 37 °C
