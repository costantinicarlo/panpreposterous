Panpreposterous — build reproducible PDFs with Pandoc + XeLaTeX

Usage:
  panpreposterous INPUT.md [-o output.pdf] [--csl style.csl] [--bibliography refs.bib] [extra pandoc args...]

Defaults:
  template: /opt/panpreposterous/template/preprint_template_xe_citeproc.tex
  filters : /opt/panpreposterous/filters/backmatter.lua, /opt/panpreposterous/filters/supplementary.lua
  engine  : xelatex
  citeproc: on

Example:
  panpreposterous manuscript.md --bibliography refs.bib --csl journal.csl -o manuscript.pdf
